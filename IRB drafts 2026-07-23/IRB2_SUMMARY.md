## Assumptions the draft makes

- Treats co-design workshops, the barriers survey, optional interviews, and consultation notes as research with instructor consent (grant T1h; PI: "a design workshop with instructors IS research").
- Deployment procedures transplant the approved course-based protocol's accepted design (matching, icebreaker, three-part agenda, pre/post + 8 longitudinal surveys over 4 years, $10/longitudinal survey), with consent administered by the research team instead of the host instructor, and host-instructor blinding until final grades.
- Comparison-course students are surveyed only, consent-by-submission, pre/post uncompensated (only follow-ups carry the $10 card).
- No grades, academic records, or classroom recordings are collected — keeps FERPA surface minimal at all sites.
- Courses taught by research-team members fall under the approved course-based protocol, not this one (boundary stated in §4).
- If a first co-design round must happen in August before this protocol is approved, those sessions would need to run as non-research design meetings (no data analyzed for research) — timing risk worth discussing with Greg.
