## Appendix G: Instructor recruitment texts

### Mailing-list blurb (institutional and professional mailing lists)

Subject: Reflection groups and reflection on AI use in your course — NSF-funded, materials and support provided

Colleagues — our NSF CAREER-funded research team at the University of Maine works on two related teaching approaches: structured peer reflection groups, where small groups of students meet briefly with a guided agenda, which our prior work found supports professional skills like iterative improvement, communication, and psychological safety; and assignments that ask students to reflect on how they use generative AI, which our prior work found supports learning about critical AI use.

We are recruiting instructors — at UMaine and other institutions — to join a design workshop where instructors adapt these approaches to their own courses. Workshops can be about reflection groups, about integrating generative AI into a course, or about designing reflection on AI use. You can also just tell us what would make this feasible for you, through a 10–15 minute survey: ____

Working with us is separate from being in the research: you can attend a workshop and use the materials without being studied, attending does not obligate you to use anything in your course, and any research participation is voluntary and confidential.

Questions: Dr. Gregory L. Nelson (gregory.nelson@maine.edu) or Cyril Agbewali-Koku (cyril.agbewalikoku@maine.edu).
