## Why merged rather than staged

The split rested on the student arm arriving later as a cheap modification. UMaine's rules say otherwise: a modification changing **two or more** of {population, procedures, purpose} *"becomes a new study."* The student arm changes two. Staging therefore costs two full applications. Nothing in the rules requires per-population protocols, the instructions contemplate multi-population studies, and the board has already approved reflection groups in courses under the team's course protocol.
