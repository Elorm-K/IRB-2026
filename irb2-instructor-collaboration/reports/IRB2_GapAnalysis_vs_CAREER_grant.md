## Also check

### Outcome measures — defined once and referred to as "outcomes"?

**No.** IRB #1 does this correctly, in §2 Summary:

> *"For the purposes of the research "outcomes" below refers to: professional skills & dispositions, learning, career, and other personal and social outcomes. For example, professional skills (such as iterative improvement, lifelong learning, psychological safety, and communication), self-efficacy, professional identity, and other personal, professional, and group outcomes."*

**IRB #2 has no such definition.** It re-enumerates a *different* subset each time, and the term "outcome measures" appears in Appendix H, Appendix I, and Data collected 1.7–1.8 without ever being defined:

- RQ3: *"professional skills (such as iterative improvement, lifelong learning, psychological safety, and communication), self-efficacy, and professional identity"* — omits learning and career
- Appendix F / Appendix I purpose: *"your learning, self-efficacy, professional identity, and professional skills"* — omits the four named professional skills
- §8 Benefits: *"learn and reflect critically about their learning and professional skills"* — omits self-efficacy and identity

**Recommendation:** copy IRB #1's definition sentence into IRB #2 §2, then use the single word "outcomes" in RQ3, §2 items 9–10, §8, Appendix H, Appendix I, and the consent forms.
