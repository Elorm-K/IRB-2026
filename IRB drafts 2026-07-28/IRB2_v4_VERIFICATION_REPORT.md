## Addendum 2 — compensation corrected to a one-time payment (2026-07-28)

Per the PI: the $25 is paid **once** for opting into an interview, not once per interview. Applied in Section 9, Appendix B, and the Appendix G interview invitation; verified 0 occurrences of the previous per-interview language. This closes the first half of the open compensation decision.

Three consequences worth the PI's attention:

1. **Divergence from IRB #1.** IRB #1 Section 9 currently reads *"Participants who complete an interview will be offered a $25 electronic VISA gift card"* for **each** interview. Two protocols now reach the same board with different rules for the same activity. Either confirm the difference is intended, or align them — flagged in the Section 9 placeholder.
2. **Repeat interviews are unpaid.** The protocol invites follow-up interviews in later semesters (Section 2 item 5). A second 30–60 minute interview at no additional payment averages below the ~$25-per-interview level `board-rules.md` §4 records as this board's floor for an interview of that length. Defensible for a one-off, but if repeat interviews become common, expect the board to raise it.
3. **Tax-reporting language is now unnecessary.** Maximum total compensation per instructor under this protocol is $25, so no participant can reach the $75 threshold that triggers the required HR/Purchasing language from the informed consent checklist. Section 9 states this explicitly so a reviewer does not have to work it out.
