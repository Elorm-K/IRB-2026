## 2. What the policy actually says (research)

Two separate UMaine rules were being conflated:

1. **[ORA gift-card policy](https://umaine.edu/ora/gift-card-purchases/)** —
   *"Gift cards may not be used to compensate employees."* Unconditional, and per-card
   cap of $50. This restricts the **instrument**, not the compensation. (Recorded with
   exact quotes in `references/institutions/umaine.md` §7, from the 2026-08-07 IRB #2
   review round.)
2. **[Human Subject Payment Guidelines](https://umaine.edu/research-compliance/human-subjects/human-subject-payment-guidelines/)
   (Office of Research Compliance)** — presupposes that employees *do* receive research
   payments: a **one-time payment over $75** to a UMaine employee is reported to Human
   Resources, which decides whether it is added to gross wages and taxed. Non-employees
   route through Purchasing (Form 1099 at $600+/year).

So: UMaine employees **can** be compensated for research participation; the payment just
can't take gift-card form. At this protocol's amounts ($25 workshop/interview, $10
survey) no single payment approaches $75, so no tax-reporting language is triggered —
per the keep-it-simple rule, don't add any.

**One item to confirm with Aubrey/business office** (not blocking the draft): the exact
instrument for an employee payment (payroll addition vs. an Accounts Payable check). The
drafted wording below is deliberately instrument-agnostic ("through the University's
regular payment process") so it stays true either way and never needs a mod.

## 3. Drafted language (short), per location

### §9 Narrative — replace the employee paragraph

> Instructors employed by the University of Maine receive the same compensation.
> Because University policy does not permit gift cards to be used to compensate
> University employees, their compensation is issued through the University's regular
> payment process instead of a gift card.

### Appendix A consent form — replace the employee sentence

> If you are an employee of the University of Maine, you will receive the same
> compensation, but because University policy does not permit gift cards to be used to
> compensate employees, your payment will be issued through the University's regular
> payment process instead of a gift card.

### Recruitment emails — employee parenthetical (replaces "…who take part without compensation")

> (University of Maine employees receive the same amount through a University payment
> instead of a gift card)

### Recruitment emails — missing compensation lines

- **Mailing-list blurb** and **targeted email** (they invite to workshop + survey), one
  sentence after the survey link:

  > You will receive $25 for taking part in a workshop and $10 for the survey
  > (University of Maine employees receive the same amounts through a University
  > payment instead of a gift card).

- **Workshop invitation** — already states $25; append the employee parenthetical.
- **Follow-up survey email** — keeps its $10 sentence; swap the parenthetical for the
  employee one above.
- **Interview invitation** — keeps its $25 sentence; swap the parenthetical.
- **Consultation invitation** — unchanged ("no compensation for consultations").

### Payment-information form intro (if the doc carries the IRB #2-style Appendix)

> Because University policy does not permit gift cards to be used to compensate
> University employees, instructors employed by the University of Maine receive their
> compensation through the University's regular payment process instead of a gift card.
